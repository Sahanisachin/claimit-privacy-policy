Privacy Policy – Lost & Found App for LPU

Last Updated: November 24, 2026

ClaimIT ("we", "our", or "us") is a Lost and Found application designed to help students within the Lovely Professional University (LPU) community report, discover, and claim lost or found items.

This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected, how it is used, how it is stored, and the choices available to users.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use ClaimIT, we may collect:

  • Account information: Name, email address, authentication identifiers, and account details provided during registration.
  • College verification information: College identification or institutional details required to verify eligibility to use the app.
  • Lost and found reports: Item names, descriptions, categories, locations, report dates, and claim-related information.
  • Photographs: Images uploaded by users when reporting lost or found items.
  • App and device information: Technical information such as device type, operating system, app version, and diagnostic information where available.
  • Notification information: Information required to deliver relevant notifications about reports and claims.

2. How We Use Your Information

Information may be used to:

  • Create and manage student accounts.
  • Verify eligible college users.
  • Publish and display lost and found reports.
  • Help users identify and recover lost belongings.
  • Process item claims and ownership requests.
  • Send notifications about reports and claim updates.
  • Prevent spam, fraudulent activity, and misuse.
  • Maintain application security and functionality.
  • Respond to support and privacy requests.

3. Firebase and Third-Party Services

ClaimIT uses Google Firebase services for application infrastructure and data management, where configured. These services may include:

  • Firebase Authentication for account authentication.
  • Firebase Realtime Database for application data.
  • Firebase Cloud Storage for uploaded photographs.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging for push notifications.

Information processed through these services is subject to the applicable Google Firebase privacy and security practices.

Firebase Privacy Information: Firebase Privacy and Security

4. Visibility of User Information

ClaimIT is intended for the verified LPU student community. Information associated with published reports may be visible to other eligible users.

Depending on the application's features, administrators may access information necessary to manage reports, investigate misuse, resolve claims, and maintain application operations.

Users should avoid including passwords, sensitive personal information, or unnecessary private details in report descriptions and photographs.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell users' personal information.

Information may be disclosed when necessary:

  • To provide application functionality through Firebase.
  • To authorised administrators for app operations.
  • To comply with applicable laws or legal requests.
  • To investigate fraud, abuse, or security incidents.

6. Data Security

We use appropriate technical and organisational measures intended to protect information against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure, or loss.

However, no electronic storage system or internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Data Retention

Information is retained for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain account functionality, resolve claims, prevent misuse, and meet applicable legal obligations.

When information is no longer required, it should be deleted or anonymised in accordance with applicable retention practices.

9. Children's Privacy

ClaimIT is designed for the intended college student community and is not specifically directed toward children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children where prohibited by applicable law.

10. User Responsibilities

Users are responsible for:

  • Providing accurate registration information.
  • Keeping their account credentials confidential.
  • Uploading photographs and content they are authorised to share.
  • Using the application lawfully and responsibly.
  • Not uploading misleading, abusive, or unlawful content.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when application features, data practices, or legal requirements change. Updated versions will be published on this page with a revised date.
